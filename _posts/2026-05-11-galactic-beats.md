This project was my second Unity module at university, so I wanted to establish my understanding of the engine by making a technically challenging project with difficult systems programming.
Making this project helped me understand my limits in the Unity Engine and so scoping future Unity projects became significantly more manageable.

The main system involves playing the song and lining up a metronome in the background which spawns an enemy a quarter beat before the beat is sounded in the song. 
Originally, I intended to use waveform analysis to automatically place enemies whenever the song would have an audio spike. However, I decided to cut this concept in the interest of scope, instead, enemies can be assigned to any beat in the song.

Similarly to Pinball Trials, and being an arcade game based project, this project relies on high scores to provide the player replayability. 

During the project I was pushed to grow my problem solving and debugging skill as the concepts I was exploring had very little documentation.
I came out of the project more confident in not only using Unity for future projects, but also independent learning and analytical thinking for future projects.
